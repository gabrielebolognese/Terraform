# Terraform - the player's manual

*Make Mars a living world.*

Terraform is a game about turning Mars, a cold, airless desert, into a planet people can live on
in the open. From orbit you order the great machines that change a world: mirrors that warm it,
factories that thicken its air, comets that bring it water. On the ground you found cities, build
them up one structure at a time, and link them by rail. The planet changes slowly and for real:
the air thickens, the ice melts, the first water pools, life takes hold, and one day the air is
breathable.

This manual describes the game as it is today. What changed in each update is in the
[update notes](update-notes.md).

## The game in one minute

1. **From the title screen, choose New world**, give it a name, and press **Create world**.
2. **You start in orbit**, looking at a bare red Mars. The panel on the left tells you what the
   planet needs next. Order an **Orbital mirror array** to start warming it.
3. **Found a city**: press **Found a city**, then click the planet where it should stand. Open it
   to build habitats, power, water, oxygen and food, and people will come.
4. **Keep both going.** The planet's machines cost credits; your cities' research earns more. As
   the planet passes each phase, new machines unlock, until Mars is a living world.

A full terraforming is a long game: several days of play at normal speed. Cities move faster,
with buildings that go up in seconds or minutes at first and take hours at their highest levels.
