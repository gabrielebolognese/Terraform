# Earth: trade, shipments and contracts

Earth is a long way off, but it is part of your game. Whenever Mars and Earth line up, a launch
window opens. Then your Spaceports' rockets fly to Earth with what you sell, and come back with
what you ordered. Earth sells you materials, water, food and oxygen, and later settlers and
rovers. It buys back what your cities can spare, and it offers contracts that pay well if you
deliver on time. All of it is paid in the same credits as everything else.

Earth trades with **Standard** and **Terraformed start** worlds made from Update 34 on. A
**Sandbox** has no use for it: everything there is free. Worlds made before this update have no
trade with Earth either.

## The Earth button

**Earth** sits in the bar beside **Statistics** and **Alerts**: at the bottom right in orbit, at the
top right in a city. When Earth has a contract on offer, the button reads
**Earth (an offer)**. The panel shows, from the top:

- **Your reputation** with Earth, 0 to 100, and a word for it (Distrusted, Doubted, Neutral,
  Respected, Trusted). A new world starts at 50, Neutral.
- **The next launch window**: how long until it opens at the speed you are playing. It also says
  how long a rocket's trip to Earth and back takes.
- **Your bank**, and your trade so far: credits spent on Earth's goods, credits Earth has paid for
  yours, and credits from contracts.
- **Earth's prices**: what Earth asks for each good and what it pays for one.
- **An order** for the next launch window.
- **Orders waiting** for the window, and **rockets away**, with when each is home.
- **Contracts**: Earth's offer, and the ones you have taken.
- **Record**: the latest launches, landings, missed windows and contracts, newest first. **Show**
  takes you to the city.

## Launch windows

Mars and Earth line up about every 26 months, and a window opens each time:

- **Standard pace:** about every **9 and a half minutes** at 1x, the first one about 9 and a half
  minutes into a new world.
- **Classic pace:** about every **71 seconds** at 1x. The planet runs eight times faster there, and
  so does its calendar.

At each window, every city with something to send or fetch flies **its rocket**: the rocket of its
first finished Spaceport whose rocket is at home. It is the rocket you see on the pad. It climbs
out of sight with a roar, with what goes to Earth, and comes back down with what you ordered: about
**3 minutes 12 seconds** later at Standard pace, about **25 seconds** at Classic. It is always
home before the next window.

**A missed window waits for the next.** If a city's rocket is away when the window opens, for
example on a supply run (**Launch rocket**), its orders wait for the next window. The same goes
if its only Spaceport is still being built. The record and an alert say so. Every city is founded
with a Spaceport, so a new city can trade from its first window.

## Buying and selling

To place an order, choose a **city** (any city with a Spaceport), a **good** and **how many**:

- **Buy for** *N* **credits**: you pay now, at the price on the button. It goes up at the next
  window and lands with the rocket. **Cancel (refund)** takes it back, with every credit, until it
  goes.
- **Sell**: at the next window the rocket takes up to that many, and Earth pays its price at that
  window. The button shows about what it would fetch today. A sale with nothing to take waits for
  the next window.

Below the buttons the panel says how much room the city has for the good now, and how much a
rocket could send.

**What a rocket takes.** A rocket never takes a store below a quarter of its room. That goes for a
sale and for a contract alike, so your city keeps a reserve of water, food and oxygen.

**What lands.** Goods go into the city's stores, as far as they have room. Whatever does not fit
is sold straight back to Earth at its price, which is less than you paid, so mind the room shown
under the order. Settlers move into empty homes; one with no home to go to stays on Earth, and
you get back what you paid for them. A rover joins the city's own and goes out on jobs like the
others.

### Earth's prices

| Good | Earth asks, about | Earth pays, about | For sale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Materials | 4 credits each | 1.60 | From the start |
| Water | 6 | 2.40 | From the start |
| Food | 6 | 2.40 | From the start |
| Oxygen | 6 | 2.40 | From the start |
| Settlers | 30 each | Not bought | Once you have met 1 contract |
| Rovers | 1,500 each | Not bought | Once you have met 3 contracts |

Prices move:

- **With Earth's supply.** Each window, each good can be up to a fifth cheaper or dearer than
  usual. A good that is dear to buy also fetches more. Two worlds made from the same seed see the
  same swings in supply.
- **With your trade.** The more you buy of a good, the dearer it gets: about double after 2,000
  credits' worth. The more you sell, the less Earth pays. Each window the market forgets half of
  what it remembers. A big order is priced at its middle, so buying it in one go or in parts costs
  the same.
- **With your reputation.** At 100, Earth asks a quarter less and pays a quarter more. At 0 it is
  the other way round.

Earth never pays more for a good than it asks for one, so buying and selling back never makes
credits.

## Contracts

At each launch window, once one of your cities has people, Earth makes an **offer**:

- **Materials**: 150 to 310 at Standard pace (20 to 40 at Classic).
- **Water**: 40 to 80 at Standard pace (10 at Classic).
- **Research**: as much as your laboratories, observatories and research forums earn in 3 to 6 of
  the planet's years: about 13 to 26 minutes of their work at 1x at Standard pace. Earth only asks
  for research once something of yours earns it.

The offer says what it pays and when it is due: **by the third launch window after it was
offered**, about 28 minutes away at Standard pace. It stands until the next window, when Earth
replaces it with a new one. Press **Accept** to take it. You can have several under way.

**Sending.** Materials and water go up on the rockets at each window, from any of your cities
whose rocket flies, as far as each holds more than a quarter of its room. Research goes by radio:
from the moment you accept, the research your planet earns goes to Earth instead of your bank,
until the contract is met. Each contract under way shows how much has been sent, with a bar, and
how long is left.

**Met:** Earth pays at once:

- 4 credits for each material;
- 6 for each unit of water;
- 2 for each credit of research.

Your reputation rises by 10. **Failed** (still short when its window comes): nothing is paid,
nothing sent comes back, and your reputation falls by 20.

Meeting contracts unlocks Earth's settlers (after 1) and rovers (after 3), and good standing makes
everything cheaper. A contract you cannot meet costs more than leaving it.

## Alerts

Two pop up, and stay in **Alerts**:

- **Launch window missed in** *city*: its rocket was away, or it has no finished Spaceport.
- **Earth's** *materials* **contract failed**: it was not met in time.

Two go in **Alerts** without popping up:

- **Rocket home in** *city*, with what it landed.
- **Earth's** *materials* **contract met**, with what Earth paid.

Clicking any of them opens **Earth**. A rocket taking off is only in the panel's record, and an
offer is shown by the Earth button.

## While you are away

Launch windows keep coming while the game is closed. Orders go up and come back, and contracts are
met or fail, just as if you were watching. The **While you were away** summary says how many
contracts were met and failed ("Earth: a contract met, a contract failed - see Earth").

## The Spaceport

Select a Spaceport to see what its rocket is doing. At home, it reminds you that it flies at each
launch window, and that a supply run away at a window misses it. Away to Earth, it says when it
will be home.
