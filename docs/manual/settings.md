# Settings

**Settings** on the title screen, or **Menu** then **Settings** in a world, opens every setting in
one panel: **Sound**, **Graphics**, **Controls**, **Interface** and **Saving**. A change works at
once - you hear it, see it or feel it straight away - and nothing you set here changes your world:
the planet and your cities play exactly the same whatever you choose.

Your settings are yours, not a world's. This browser keeps them for every world you play, apart
from the worlds themselves: they are not in a world's save or its exported file, and deleting a
world leaves them as they are.

## Sound

A volume for everything together, the music, the ambience, the effects and the interface, and
**Mute all sound**. See [Sound and music](sound.md#sound-settings).

## Graphics

| Setting | Choices | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Detail in a city** | **Auto**, **High**, **Medium**, **Low** | How much detail a city is drawn with. **Auto** (to start with) draws full detail close in and less as you zoom out over a large city. The others hold one level whatever the zoom. **High**, **Medium** and **Low** each show what a frame of the city took to draw on your computer, once you have seen a city at that level ("about 6.2 ms a frame here"). |
| **Pictures of the city kept** | **Fewer** (up to 48 MB), **Standard** (up to 96 MB), **More** (up to 144 MB) | A city is drawn as pictures, square by square, and the game keeps the ones you have seen. More pictures make coming back to a place smoother and use more memory. |
| **Moving details** | on or off | Lights blinking, turbine blades turning, crowds and steam. Off, every building stands still as it is, which is lighter to draw; rovers, trains and a rocket taking off or landing still move. Next to it, what a frame took with them on and off at High. |

## Controls

| Setting | To start with | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Camera speed** | 1x | How fast the keys and the edge of the screen move the view: 0.5x to 3x |
| **Edge scrolling** | off | In a city, the view moves when the pointer rests at the edge of the screen |
| **Invert zoom** | off | The mouse wheel zooms the other way, on the planet, in a city, in the planner and on the world map |
| **Trackpad: two fingers move the map** | off | Two fingers sliding move the city, the planner and the world map (and turn the planet), and a pinch zooms. Off, the wheel zooms, as a mouse wheel does |

A trackpad's pinch always zooms, whatever these say. On a touch screen, one finger does what the
mouse does, and a pinch with two zooms, in every view.

### Keys

Every keyboard shortcut is listed under **Keys**, two keys to an action. To change one, click the
key and press the new one: any key counts, **Esc** too, and **Ctrl**, **Alt** and **Shift** can be
part of it. **Cancel** leaves it as it was; **Clear** leaves that slot empty. A key already used by
another action is swapped: that action takes the old key, and the panel says so. **Reset keys** puts
every key back. Keys are named by where they sit on the keyboard, so W, A, S and D are the same
four keys whatever your keyboard's layout. See [Controls](controls.md#keyboard) for the list.

## Interface

| Setting | Choices | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Interface size** | 75% to 150% | The size of the panels, buttons and text. The planet and your cities are drawn the same. |
| **Colours** | **Standard**, **Red-green colour-blind safe**, **Blue-yellow colour-blind safe** | Changes the colours that mean something - good, warning and bad, alerts, the planner's map, its coverage, its zones' first colours and the land a city can claim - to ones that stay apart for that kind of colour blindness. |
| **Motion** | **Follow my system's setting**, **Reduced**, **Full** | With motion reduced, nothing in the panels slides, fades or pulses; going down to a city and back up is a cut instead of a flight; and the planet does not turn by itself. |
| **High contrast** | on or off | Solid black panels, white text and stronger edges. |
| **Pop-up alerts** | **Urgent alerts and notices**, **Urgent alerts only**, **None** | Which alerts pop up (and sound). **Alerts** keeps every one whatever you choose. |

Whatever the colours, nothing is told by colour alone: every alert has its word (**Urgent**,
**Notice**, **Done**), every state its words, an outpost's marker is square where a city's is
round, and the planner's map has patterns as well as colours (see
[Cities](cities.md#the-city-planner)).

## Saving

| Setting | Choices | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Autosave** | every 15 seconds (to start with), 30 seconds, minute, or 5 minutes | How often the world you are playing is saved while you play |

A world is also saved when you quit to the title, when you go down to a city or come back up, when
you switch to another tab, and when you press **Save** in the menu or **Ctrl+S**. The panel says where
your worlds are kept; see [Worlds and saving](worlds-and-saving.md#saving).
