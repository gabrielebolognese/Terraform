# Controls

The game is played with the mouse, a trackpad or a touch screen, and has keyboard shortcuts for
the things you do most. Every key below can be changed in **Settings**, under **Controls**; this
page gives the keys as they come. See [Settings](settings.md#keys).

## Keyboard

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Cancel what you are doing, close what is open, or open the game menu | **Esc** |
| Pause, or go back to the speed you had | **Space** |
| The world's first, second, third and fourth speeds | **1**, **2**, **3**, **4** |
| Save now | **Ctrl+S** (**Cmd+S** on a Mac) |
| Move the view up, left, down, right (in orbit: turn the planet) | **W**, **A**, **S**, **D**, or the arrow keys |
| Zoom in, zoom out | **=** and **-**, or **Numpad +** and **Numpad -** |
| Objectives | **O** |
| Alerts | **N** |
| Statistics | **C** |
| Earth | **E** |
| The world map (from a city, it goes up to orbit first) | **M** |
| Back to orbit, from a city | **B** |
| The city planner, and back to the city | **P** |
| Upgrade the selected building | **U** |

**Speeds.** The number keys pick from the speeds the world offers, in order, pause left out: in a
Standard world **1**, **2** and **3** are 1x, 2x and 3x; in a Classic world **1** to **4** are 1x,
10x, 100x and 1000x.

The keys move the view while they are held, at the **Camera speed** you set. Shortcuts do nothing
while you are typing in a box (a name, a seed, an amount), and **Space** on a button presses the
button. While the game menu is open, only **Esc** (to close it) and **Ctrl+S** work.

## In orbit

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Turn the planet | Drag with the left mouse button (flick to set it spinning), or the move keys |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel, a trackpad's pinch, two fingers on a touch screen, or the zoom keys |
| Go down to a settlement | Click its marker, then **Go down** (or **Open** in the Settlements list) |
| Choose where to found a settlement | After **Found a city** or **Found an outpost**, click the planet |
| Open the game menu | **Menu** (bottom right) or **Esc** |
| Open the objectives | **Objectives** (bottom right, above **Menu**) or **O** |
| See what needs you | **Alerts** (bottom right, beside **Menu**) or **N** |
| See the charts of the planet and each city | **Statistics** (bottom right, beside **Alerts**) or **C** |
| Trade with Earth, and see its contracts | **Earth** (bottom right, beside **Alerts**) or **E** |
| Go to where an alert happened | Click the alert, in **Alerts** or as it pops up |
| Dismiss a pop-up alert | **x** on it |
| Put the guide away | **Skip the guide** on its card |
| Cancel founding | **Esc** or **Cancel** |

Left alone for a few seconds, the planet turns slowly by itself - unless motion is reduced in
[Settings](settings.md#interface).

## In a city

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Move around | Drag, the move keys, two fingers on a trackpad (with that setting on), or rest the pointer at the edge of the screen (with **Edge scrolling** on) |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel, a trackpad's pinch, two fingers on a touch screen, or the zoom keys |
| Place a building | Choose its card, then click the ground |
| Select a building, a rock or a deposit | Click it |
| Show the ground's deposits | **Deposits** (top right, with the tools); choosing a mine, water extractor, geothermal plant or solar array shows its own |
| Upgrade the selected building | **Upgrade** in its panel, or **U** |
| Raise the selected building's ground, against the sea | **Raise its ground** in its panel (where the sea floods) |
| Move a threatened city's people to safety | **Evacuate its people**, in **The sea** in the city's panel |
| Lay corridors, cables or railway | Choose the tool, then drag; start on an existing link to remove links |
| Put down the chosen tool, or deselect | **Esc** |
| Scroll the build bar | Mouse wheel over it, or drag its scrollbar |
| Back to orbit | **Back to orbit**, or **B** |
| The city planner | **City planner**, or **P** |
| Open the game menu | **Menu** (top right) |
| Open the objectives | **Objectives** (top right, under **Menu**) or **O** |
| See what needs you, or the charts | **Alerts** and **Statistics** (top right, beside **Menu**), or **N** and **C** |
| Trade with Earth | **Earth** (top right, beside **Alerts**) or **E** |

## In the city planner

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Use the chosen tool | Drag with the left mouse button |
| Move around | Drag with the middle mouse button (or choose **Move**), the move keys, or two fingers on a trackpad (with that setting on) |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel, a trackpad's pinch, two fingers on a touch screen, or the zoom keys |
| See what is on a tile | Point at it: the line under the map names it |
| Show where a service reaches | Click **Green space**, **Medical care**, **Research**, **Transport** or **Shops** in the **Coverage** row; **Off** hides it (a world made from Update 32 on) |
| Back to the city | **City view**, or **P** |

## On the world map

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Move around | Drag, the move keys, or two fingers on a trackpad (with that setting on) |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel, from the whole planet down to a single city's land; a trackpad's pinch; two fingers on a touch screen; or the zoom keys |
| Select a settlement | Click it |
| Cancel laying a railway | **Esc** |
| Close the map | **Back to orbit**, or **M** |

## Game speed

The speed buttons sit at the top of the orbit panel.

| Pace or mode | Speeds |
|---|---|
| Standard pace | pause, 1x, 2x, 3x |
| Classic pace | pause, 1x, 10x, 100x, 1000x |
| Sandbox | every speed |

Opening the game menu pauses the world; closing it goes back to the speed you had.

## The game menu

**Resume**, **Save**, **Settings** and **Save and quit to title**. On the Example planet nothing is
saved, so the last button is just **Quit to title**.

Under **Save** the menu says when the world was last saved and where your worlds are kept.
**Settings** opens every setting under it - sound, graphics, controls, the interface and saving -
and closes them again. The same settings are on the title screen. See [Settings](settings.md).
