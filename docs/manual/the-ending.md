# Winning: victory, score and achievements

Mars can be won. A Standard world (made from Update 39 on) is won when the planet holds a living
world, and every world keeps a score and earns achievements on the way. Winning one world opens
**new game plus**: a bonus for your next.

Sandbox worlds and Terraformed starts have no victory, score or achievements: a sandbox has
nothing to win, and a Terraformed start begins on a living planet. Worlds made before Update 39
keep playing as they did, without them.

## Victory

The planet must reach **Living world** - all six minimums met at once: warm enough, thick enough
air, breathable oxygen, little carbon dioxide, oceans covering 30-70% of the planet, and a thriving
biosphere (see [The planet](the-planet.md#the-phases)) - and **hold all six for 10 years in a row**.
That is about 44 minutes at 1x at the Standard pace (about 5 and a half at Classic). If any of the
six slips out of its band, the 10 years start again. Once won, a world stays won.

A full terraforming at the Standard pace takes several days of play: a playthrough that orders the
planet's machines on a steady schedule wins after about 127 hours at 1x (about 42 at 3x).

When your world is won, the next moment you look at it:

- the panels step aside and the camera swings round the living planet as **A living world** comes
  up (with reduced motion on, the camera stays still);
- a few seconds later, a card tells **the world's story**: the year it was won and how long that is
  at 1x, the cities founded and still standing, the most people the planet held and how many live
  there now, who and what the sea took, the cities held behind their Seawall and the people moved
  to safety, the pressure you put on the planet, the credits taken in, Earth's contracts met, and
  the achievements earned;
- below it, **its score**, part by part;
- **Keep playing** goes back to the world - nothing ends, the planet and your cities carry on - and
  **Save and back to the title** saves it and returns to the title screen.

The sequence plays once in each world. A world won while you were away plays it when you next open
it, and the **While you were away** summary in the Instruments drawer says "The planet held its
living world: you have won."

## The score

Every world made from Update 39 on in Standard mode keeps a score, from its first minute. It is
worked out from the world as it stands, so it is the same whether the time passed while you played
or while you were away.

| Part | Points |
|---|---|
| **Speed** | Once won: 1,000 for a win in year 1,720, more for sooner (a win in year 1,544 scores 1,114), less for later. Nothing before the win |
| **People** | 1 for every 10 people, at the most the planet has held at once |
| **Cities held by their Seawall** | +250 for each city standing with the sea 10 m or more over its ground - where it would have been lost - behind its Seawall |
| **Cities lost to the sea** | -250 for each city (or metropolis) the sea has taken |
| **Pressure on the planet** | -4 for every 1,000 machine-years: each machine of the planet's - a mirror, a factory, an importer, a shield, a scrubber, a shade - for each year it was online |

The score never goes below 0. The **Worlds** list shows it under each world: "Score 865 - won in
year 1,721 - 14 achievements", or "not won yet". A playthrough that orders its machines on a
steady schedule runs about 96,000 machine-years by its win (a pressure of -385); one that takes
the shield away and the mirrors down once the planet warms itself wins with about 63,000.

## Achievements

There are 30, each earned the moment the world shows it, and none can be earned in your first
minute. When one is earned a card says so, with its chime. Those marked **rare** reward how you
won rather than how long you played.

**Achievements** on the title screen shows all 30 as a map: a lane for each group below, running
left to right from the landing, each achievement a box with its picture and name. The ones you
have earned are lit green with a tick; the rest are grey with a lock. Point at a box, or move to it
with **Tab**, and it opens to say what earns it, whether you have earned it and the world it was
first earned in. The rare ones have a double edge. Turn the mouse wheel over the map to move
along it. They are yours, not a world's: deleting a world does not take back what
you earned in it. They are kept by this browser, like your settings.

**The planet**

| Achievement | How to earn it |
|---|---|
| The caps let go | The planet reaches Phase 2, runaway thickening |
| Open water | The planet reaches Phase 3: liquid water at the surface |
| Green at the water's edge | The planet reaches Phase 4, ecopoiesis |
| Blue sky | The planet reaches Phase 5, oxygenation and buffer |
| Half an atmosphere | The air reaches 500 mbar at the surface |
| A breath of air | Oxygen reaches 100 mbar: enough to breathe |
| An ocean | The sea covers 30% of the planet |
| A living world | The planet reaches Phase 6: all six minimums at once |
| Home | Win: all six minimums held for 10 years in a row |

**Cities**

| Achievement | How to earn it |
|---|---|
| A hundred people | 100 people living on the planet |
| A thousand people | 1,000 people living on the planet |
| A people | 4,000 people living on the planet |
| A great structure | A Skyscraper, Medical Center, Observatory, Station or Mega Mall stands finished: the great structures that ask for a city of 1,000 people or more |
| Room to grow | Land claimed beyond a city's founding ground |
| A network | 3 cities on one rail network, with a city of 500 people |
| A metropolis | A metropolis founded |
| A thousand years | A city still standing in the year 1,000 |

**Earth and the bank**

| Achievement | How to earn it |
|---|---|
| A fortune | 25,000 credits taken in, all told (a world starts with 3,000) |
| Lift-off | A rocket lifts off for Earth |
| Delivered | An Earth contract met |
| Trusted by Earth | Earth's regard reaches 90 |

**Events**

| Achievement | How to earn it |
|---|---|
| Decisive | An event answered before its time ran out |
| Steady hands | 5 events answered before their time ran out |

**The sea**

| Achievement | How to earn it |
|---|---|
| Against the sea | A Seawall standing in a city as the sea comes within 20 m of its ground |
| Everyone out | People moved to safety from a city the sea threatens |
| Held the line (rare) | A city standing with the sea 10 m or more over its ground - where it would be lost - behind its Seawall |

**How it was won** (all rare)

| Achievement | How to earn it |
|---|---|
| Nobody lost to the sea | Win with at least 3 cities standing, and no settlement and no one ever lost to the sea |
| A light touch | Win with fewer than 70,000 machine-years |
| Swift | Win by the year 1,600 |
| Self-reliant | Win without ever buying anything from Earth. Selling to Earth and its contracts do not count, nor an order you cancelled before it went up |

These four are judged at the moment your world is won, and never again: a city founded after the
win does not count towards "Nobody lost to the sea".

## New game plus

Once you have won any world, **New world** offers **New game plus**: a bonus for the new world, if
it is a Standard world. Choose one, or **No bonus**:

| Bonus | What it gives |
|---|---|
| **Funded from home** | Start with 9,000 credits instead of 3,000 |
| **A bigger garage** | Five rovers at every headquarters instead of three: more builds at once |
| **Well supplied** | Every city founded with its stores full: 400 materials instead of 250 |
| **Trusted by Earth** | Earth's regard starts at 80 instead of 50: better prices from the first launch (Earth asks about 15% less, and pays about 15% more) |

The bonus is part of the world for good, and the **Worlds** list shows it: "New game plus: A
bigger garage". A world with a bonus keeps a score and earns achievements like any other.
