# Getting started

## The title screen

When the game opens you see Mars turning slowly behind the menu:

- **Continue - *name*** opens the world you played last. With no world yet it just says
  **Continue** and cannot be pressed.
- **Worlds** lists every world you have made: play, rename, duplicate, back up or delete them.
  See [Worlds and saving](worlds-and-saving.md).
- **New world** makes a new planet to play.
- **Example planet** shows a fully terraformed Mars with cities and metropolises already built,
  to look around. It takes about forty seconds to build, and nothing you do there is saved.
- **Settings** is not available yet; sound, graphics and control settings arrive in a later
  update.
- **Credits** says who made the game.

## Making a world

Press **New world** and choose:

- **Name.** Anything you like; it starts as "World 1", "World 2" and so on.
- **Seed.** The seed decides where the continents and basins lie. The same seed always makes the
  same planet, so you can share a good one with a friend. Press **Random** for a new one; you can
  also type any word or phrase, and the same words will always make the same world.
- **Mode.**
  - **Standard** is the game: you earn what you build, and new machines and buildings unlock as
    the planet changes.
  - **Sandbox** gives unlimited materials and credits, unlocks everything, and nothing is ever
    lost. Every game speed is available. Use it to experiment.
  - **Terraformed start** puts you on a living planet with cities already built, laid out from
    your seed. It takes about forty seconds to make, and unlike the Example planet it is yours and
    is saved.
- **Pace.**
  - **Standard - real build times** (recommended). Buildings take real time: seconds for the
    smallest at first, then minutes, then hours for the largest at their highest levels. Work
    carries on while the game is closed. The planet changes slowly, over days of play.
  - **Classic - the fast game before real build times.** Everything happens much faster, and you
    get speeds up to 1000x. Worlds made before real build times arrived play at this pace.

Press **Create world**, and you are dropped straight into orbit above your new planet.

## The guide

In a new Standard world, a card at the top of the screen takes you through your first seven
steps - found a city, place power, place a dome, connect them, raise a building a level, claim
land, and lay a railway to a second settlement - and the button or card to press glows. It moves
on by itself as soon as your world shows the step done. **Skip the guide** puts it away for good.
Alongside it, **Objectives** holds goals with rewards: settlers, rovers, materials, credits and
more. See [Goals](goals.md).

## Your first half hour

1. **Look at "What the planet needs next"** on the left. At the start it asks for warmth: press
   **Order one** to order an Orbital mirror array. Orders come online over time, not at once -
   about one unit every nine minutes at normal speed - so order a few. You start with 3,000
   credits.
2. **Found your first city.** In the **Settlements** section, press **Found a city** (you can type
   a name first), then click the planet where it should stand. Founding is free. Your city lands
   with a Headquarters and a Spaceport.
3. **Open the city** with the **Open** button beside its name. The camera dives down to the
   surface.
4. **Build the basics.** Pick a structure from the build bar at the bottom and click the ground to
   place it. A rover drives out from the Headquarters and builds it. Start with:
   - a **Solar Array** for power (it is ready in about 30 seconds),
   - a **Regolith Mine** for materials, which you need for everything else,
   - a **Habitat Dome** so people have somewhere to live,
   - a **Greenhouse** for food and a **Water Extractor** for water.

   Your Headquarters already makes a little oxygen and water. Use **Corridor** and **Power cable**
   (top right) to connect buildings that do not touch: a building only runs when it is connected
   to everything it needs.

   Leave gaps between buildings, and keep clear of the garage door at the front of the
   Headquarters. Rovers drive round buildings to reach the next site, so a tightly packed city
   builds slowly, and a building no rover can reach is refused. Corridors double as roads, twice as
   fast to drive.
5. **Watch the people arrive.** Once there is housing and enough power, water, oxygen and food,
   the first settlers move in and the city starts to grow.
6. **Send rovers to rocks.** Click a rock and press **Send rover** to break it up for materials,
   and select the Spaceport and press **Launch rocket** for a supply run that comes back in about a
   minute with materials.
7. **Go back to orbit** with **Back to orbit** to keep an eye on the planet. Everything keeps
   running while you are away from a view.

## What to aim for

- **On the planet:** pass the phases one by one: Warming, Runaway thickening, First water,
  Ecopoiesis, Oxygenation and buffer, and finally Living world. See [The planet](the-planet.md).
- **On the ground:** grow your cities. Bigger cities claim more land, unlock taller buildings,
  and earn the research credits that pay for the planet's machines. See [Cities](cities.md).
