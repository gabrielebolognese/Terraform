# Goals: the guide, objectives and milestones

A Standard world gives you a hand through your first hour, and always has a next goal waiting,
with a reward for reaching it.

## The guide

In a new **Standard** world, a card at the top of the screen walks you through your first steps,
one at a time:

1. **Found a city**
2. **Place power**
3. **Place a dome**
4. **Connect them**
5. **Raise a building a level**
6. **Claim land**
7. **Lay a railway to a second settlement**

The card says which step you are on ("Step 3 of 7"), what to do from where you are looking, and
the button or card to press **glows**. It follows what is really in your world, not which buttons
you pressed: a step counts as soon as your world shows it done, however you did it, and the card
moves on by itself. Steps count in order; if you do a later one early, it counts the moment the
ones before it are done.

The first five steps take a minute or two. **Claim land** waits until your city has 200 people,
which takes most players 40 minutes to an hour: keep your people supplied with power, water,
oxygen and food, and build domes as they fill. The objectives below pay settlers that help.

Press **Skip the guide** to put it away. Once skipped or finished, it never comes back in that
world. Sandbox and Terraformed start worlds have no guide, and neither do worlds made before
Update 30.

## Objectives

Press **Objectives** to open the objectives: in orbit it is at the bottom right, just above
**Menu**; in a city, at the top right, just under **Menu**. When a reward is waiting the button says so, for example **Objectives (1 to claim)**.

There are three lists. Each shows its current goal: exactly what counts, how far along you are,
and what it pays. When a goal is done, press **Claim** to take the reward; the next goal in that
list then opens. The goals after the current one are shown as **Next**.

### Your city

| Goal | What counts | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| A hundred people | 100 people living in one city | 50 settlers from Earth |
| Steady supply | 20 years in a row (about 11 minutes at 1x) in one city of at least 200 people, with nothing running short: no blackout, thirst, hunger or thin air. A shortage starts the count again | 75 settlers from Earth |
| A town of 500 | 500 people living in one city | 2 more rovers, for good |
| A city of 1,000 | 1,000 people living in one city | 2,000 credits, and you may found a **metropolis** |

### The planet

| Goal | What counts | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| The caps let go | The planet reaches Phase 2, Runaway thickening | 1,000 credits |
| Thicker air | The air reaches 100 mbar at the surface | 2,000 credits |
| First water | The planet reaches Phase 3, First water | 3,000 credits |

### The network

| Goal | What counts | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Joined by rail | A railway between two of your settlements | 200 materials |
| Three cities joined | 3 cities on one rail network. Outposts may carry the line; only cities count | 2,000 credits, and gold markers for your cities |

### How rewards arrive

- **Settlers, rovers and materials** go to your **largest city** (the one with the most people).
  Settlers move in only as far as there are empty homes, and materials only as far as the stores
  have room, so claim when there is space. Rovers stay for good: that city's Headquarters keeps
  them on top of its own 3 and its Rover Posts.
- **Credits** go to the bank, for the planet's machines.
- **A metropolis**: a **Found a metropolis** button appears in **Settlements**, beside Found a
  city. A metropolis is a city with three times a city's ground each way.
- **Gold markers**: every city's marker on the planet and on the world map turns gold.

Each reward is sized to bring the next goal closer. Playing the way the guide teaches, a steady
player reaches a hundred people at about 35 minutes, the steady supply at about 55, a town of 500
at about 70 minutes and a city of 1,000 in under two hours. The planet's goals take longer: Runaway
thickening in about an hour and a half, 100 mbar after most of a day of play, and first water after
more than a day.

No goal is done in your first minute, and progress is never stored: it is read from your world as
it stands. If your city shrinks below a goal before you claim it, the goal is no longer done.
Claims are kept with the world, and a goal can only be claimed once.

Objectives are part of new Standard worlds only. Sandbox and Terraformed start worlds have none,
and neither do worlds made before Update 30.

## Milestones

When something big happens, a card appears in the middle of the screen with a moment of light round
its edge and a short chime:

- the planet reaches a new **phase**;
- a **city** is founded;
- a **great structure** is finished: a Spaceport, Skyscraper, Medical Center, Observatory,
  Station, Research Forum, Industrial Command, Biosphere or Mega Mall;
- an **objective** is done, with its reward waiting.

Each card stays about six seconds, or press **OK**. Several at once take turns. Nothing is
announced for what was already so when you open a world.
