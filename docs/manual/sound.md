# Sound and music

Mars is heard as well as seen: music that warms as the planet does, the wind and the weather, the
hum of your cities, and a sound for everything you do and everything that finishes. All of it is
made by the game as it plays, from tones and noise, so the music never repeats exactly.

## When sound starts

Browsers keep a page silent until you have clicked, pressed a key or touched the screen, so the
game is silent until then too. Your first click on the title screen starts the music. Nothing you
missed before that plays late.

The game goes quiet while you are on another tab, and picks up again when you come back.

## Music

Each phase of the planet has its own piece:

| Phase | Piece | How it sounds |
|---|---|---|
| 0 - Barren | Cold ground | Slow, sparse and dark: long open chords in a minor key, a few notes, and glassy high notes like frost |
| 1 - Warming | Thin light | A little more movement, the first low notes underneath |
| 2 - Runaway thickening | Pressure | Faster and fuller, a bass under it |
| 3 - First water | First water | Lighter and brighter, the glassy notes fading out |
| 4 - Ecopoiesis | Green | Warm and open, in a major key |
| 5 - Oxygenation and buffer | Breath | Brighter again, more notes |
| 6 - Living world | Living world | The warmest and fullest: a major key, a bass, a busy tune |

The music follows the furthest phase your planet has reached, so it never goes back. When the
planet reaches a new phase, the old piece fades out over about eight seconds as the new one fades
in. The title screen plays **Cold ground**: Mars as every world begins it.

## Ambience

Under the music, the world itself:

- **Wind.** On bare Mars it is a thin, high whisper. As the air thickens it grows louder and deeper,
  until a breathable world has a full wind. A dust storm blows harder.
- **Rain**, once there is weather: clouds over open water, on a planet above freezing.
- **The sea**, once there is one. In a city it is louder the nearer the city stands to the shore,
  and is not heard from about 1,500 m above it.
- **Machinery**, in a city: the hum of the buildings running near the middle of your view - louder
  the more of them there are, the harder they work and the nearer they are. A building that is
  offline, or still going up, is quiet.

From orbit you hear the planet from far off: its wind, rain and sea softer, and no machinery.

## Effects

| Sound | When |
|---|---|
| A thump | You place a building (a building the game refuses makes no sound) |
| Two bell notes | A building is finished |
| Three rising notes | An upgrade is done |
| A rush of air | A train goes by near the middle of your view |
| A deep roar | A rocket lifts off, on a supply run or to Earth |

In a city, these come from where they happen: louder near the middle of your view, and from the left
or the right as they are on the screen. What happens in another city, or while you are in orbit, is
heard quietly, from far off.

When many of the same thing happen at once - a burst of buildings finishing at a high speed - you
hear at most three of them.

## Alerts, milestones and buttons

| Sound | When |
|---|---|
| Two soft notes, falling | An alert pops up |
| Three quick pairs of notes | An urgent alert pops up: a blackout; water, oxygen or food running short; an event that needs your choice |
| A rising chime | A milestone card: a city founded, a great structure finished, an objective done |
| A grander chime | The planet reaches a new phase |
| A soft tick | Any button |

Alerts that are kept only for the record in **Alerts** - a building finished, an upgrade done, a
contract met - make no alert sound; a building finished and an upgrade done have their own sounds
above.

On the **Example planet** you hear its music, its wind and its cities, but not its buildings
finishing or its rockets lifting off: it is not your world.

## Sound settings

**Settings** on the title screen, or **Menu** then **Settings** in a world, opens the sound
settings. Each slider goes from 0 to 100%:

| Slider | What it sets | To start with |
|---|---|---|
| **Master volume** | Everything together | 80% |
| **Music** | The score | 60% |
| **Ambience** | Wind, rain, the sea and a city's machinery | 70% |
| **Effects** | Placing, finishing and upgrading; trains and rockets | 80% |
| **Interface** | Alerts, objectives, milestones and buttons | 70% |

**Mute all sound** silences everything at once and keeps your levels for when you turn it back on.

A change is heard at once. Your settings are yours, not a world's: they are kept by this browser
for every world you play, and are not part of a world's save or its exported file.

A browser with no sound at all simply plays the game silently.
